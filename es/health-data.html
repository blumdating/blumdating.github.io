Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

En resumen

  • For people in the USA: the health data Blum collects, why, who receives it, and your rights under Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar state laws.
  • Your disability and health answer is optional and needs your consent. We never sell health data.

Health data we collect, and why

  • Your disability and health answer, for example dwarfism, being a wheelchair user or “No disability”: to suggest people who share your experience and to apply the “Show me” and “Who can see me” choices. It’s shown on your profile only if you choose.
  • Your answer about how much it matters to you that a partner has a similar disability: to suggest people you’re likely to get on with. It’s never shown to anyone.
  • Anything about your health that you choose to write in your profile or your messages, or that your photos show: to show your profile to other members and deliver your messages.
  • If you choose to verify your profile, your phone compares your face with your photos. The images never leave your phone: we receive only whether you passed.

Where it comes from

From you, through the app. We don’t buy health data or get it from anyone else.

Health data we share, and with whom

  • Other Blum members: your disability and health answer, only if you choose to show it on your profile, and what you write in your profile or messages.
  • Our service providers (processors), under contracts that let them use it only to run Blum for us: our hosting and database providers, which store it, and Anthropic, which receives the text of profiles, including any health details their authors wrote, to help order profiles and translate texts. Anthropic never receives your disability and health answer.
  • Public authorities, when the law requires it.

We have no affiliates, and we never sell health data.

Your consent

We collect your disability and health answer and your answer about a partner’s disability only with your consent, given with an “I agree” tick that is never pre-ticked.

Showing your disability and health answer to other members is a separate consent, given apart from the one to collect it: choosing “Show it on my profile” under “Who sees it”. Until you choose it, your answer is “Only used for matching”: other members don’t see it and can’t find you by it. Once you choose it, they see it as tags on your profile and can find you with “Show me”. You can switch back at any time in Edit profile.

If we ever wanted to share health data for anything beyond the service you asked for, we would first ask for your separate consent.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your health data, see it, and get a list of everyone we have shared it with, with a way to contact them: Settings → Privacy → Download my data, or write to us.
  • Withdraw your consent: delete your health answer in Settings → Privacy, and change your partner answer in your matching questions.
  • Delete your health data: in Settings → Privacy, or delete your account in Settings. We delete it from our service providers too, and from backups within 30 days.

You can also use these rights by writing to blum.dating.app@gmail.com: Afonso Miguel Martins Delgado dos Santos and Duarte Miguel Bandeira Lisboa Bento, Rua da Nascente, 14, Fonte Lima, 2530-728 Santa Bárbara, Lourinhã, Portugal. It’s free, and we’ll answer within 45 days (if we need longer, we’ll tell you why within that time, and we can take at most 45 more days). We may ask you to confirm who you are.

If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; someone who wasn’t involved will look at it again within 45 days and tell you the result. If you’re still not satisfied, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General (www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint) or the attorney general where you live.
