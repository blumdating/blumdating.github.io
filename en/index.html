Dating made for people with disabilities.

Meet someone who gets it, wherever they are.

  • Made for you

    You choose whether your profile shows a disability, and who can see it. Filters only find what people chose to show.

  • Safe to meet

    Face verification on your phone, and a way to report or block someone on every profile and chat.

  • Private

    No ads, and we never sell your data. Your health answer is optional, hidden unless you show it, and you can delete it at any time.

  • For everyone

    Works with screen readers, large text and reduced motion, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
