Delete your Blum account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. It can’t be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Blum and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear at the top).
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, then Delete my account.

Without the app

If you can’t use the app, email blum.dating.app@gmail.com from your account’s email address (your Google address, if you sign in with Google), with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll check it’s your account, delete it within one month, and tell you when it’s done.

Write the email

What is deleted

Straight away: your profile, photos, answers (including your health answer), likes, matches and messages. From our backups: within 30 days.

What we keep

  • Reports and moderation decisions, for 12 months after a case is closed, so that someone who was banned can’t simply come back.
  • Purchase records, for as long as tax law requires.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play too.

Only some of your data?

Your health answer can be deleted on its own in Settings → Privacy, and photos in Edit profile. To ask us to delete anything else, email blum.dating.app@gmail.com.
