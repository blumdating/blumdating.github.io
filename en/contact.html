Report illegal content

You don’t need an account. Email us and tell us:

  • where it is: the person’s first name, age and city as Blum shows them, or a screenshot
  • why you think it’s illegal
  • your name and email address (not needed when reporting child sexual abuse material)
  • that you believe what you’re telling us is accurate and complete

Write the report

Members can also report someone straight from their profile or a chat in the app.

In an emergency

If someone is in danger, call 112 (911 in the USA and Canada).
