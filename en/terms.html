Who can use Blum

  • You’re 18 or older.
  • You’re allowed to use a dating service where you live, and you haven’t been banned from Blum before.
  • You’ve never been convicted of a sexual or violent offence.
  • You have one account, and it’s yours alone.

Your account

  • Use your own photos and your true age. Don’t pretend to be someone else.
  • Keep your sign-in safe. You’re responsible for what happens in your account.
  • The verified badge means a person passed a live check and looked like their photos. It isn’t a background check.

Respect: the community rules

Blum is a place for people with disabilities, little people and anyone who wants to date them. Everyone must feel safe here.

  • Treat people with respect. No harassment, threats, insults or hate, including about disability, dwarfism, height, bodies, health, race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • Don’t treat anyone’s disability, height or body as a fetish, and don’t push anyone to talk about their health or body.
  • Never use slurs or outdated, hurtful words for disability or dwarfism.
  • No sexual messages or images unless the other person has clearly said they want them, and never on your profile. Sending sexual images nobody asked for, or sharing someone’s intimate images without their consent, is banned, and in many countries it’s a crime.
  • No scams, spam, selling, advertising, or asking anyone for money.
  • Don’t share anything private about someone else, including what they’ve told you about their health.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that involves anyone under 18.
  • Don’t misuse Blum: no bots, no copying other people’s profiles, and no getting around limits, blocks or bans.

Child safety

Blum has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Anyone under 18 is banned from Blum. If we find content that sexualises or endangers a child, we remove it, ban the account and report it to the police and the authorities responsible, including, where the law requires it, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the USA and Cybertip.ca in Canada.

If you see anything that involves a child, report it in the app or write to our child safety contact at blum.dating.app@gmail.com.

Safety

Blum does not run criminal background checks on its members, and we can’t promise that people are who they say they are. When you meet someone, meet in a public place, tell a friend where you’ll be, and arrange your own way home. Our safety tips, in Settings → Safety, explain more and list places to get help.

Report anyone who breaks these rules, from their profile or the chat. Blocking someone stops them seeing you or contacting you.

If you’re in danger, call the emergency number: 112 anywhere in Europe, or 911 in the USA and Canada.

How we moderate

Anyone can tell us about content on Blum they believe is illegal, even without an account, at https://blumdating.github.io/en/contact.html#report or blum.dating.app@gmail.com. Members can also report from a profile or a chat. A person reviews every report.

We may remove content, limit features, warn someone, or suspend or ban an account that breaks these terms or the law, and we may act without a report when we find a serious problem. A profile reported by several different people is hidden automatically until a moderator has looked at it.

We’ll tell you what we decided about your account or content, and why, including which rule or law it’s based on and whether any part of the decision was automatic. If you think we got it wrong, write to blum.dating.app@gmail.com within six months and someone who wasn’t involved will look at it again. You can also go to court.

If we believe someone’s life or safety is at risk, we tell the police.

Your content

What you post on Blum stays yours. You allow us to store it, show it to other members and process it to run Blum, for as long as it’s on Blum. This permission ends when you delete it or your account, except for copies kept for safety, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Only post what you have the right to share.

AI suggestions

Blum uses AI to help order profiles, explain why you might match, suggest first messages and translate what people write when you ask. These are suggestions and translations can be wrong: when something matters, ask the person what they meant. You decide what to send, and you’re responsible for the messages you send.

Plans and payments

  • Blum is free to use. Plus and Gold are optional paid plans with extra features, and Super Likes and Boosts can be bought on their own. Prices, including taxes, are shown before you buy.
  • Purchases are made through Google Play and charged to your Google account.
  • Plans renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. You can cancel at any time in Google Play, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. Where the law requires it, we’ll remind you before a plan renews.
  • Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play.
  • If you live in the EEA, you can withdraw from a plan within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason. Plans start as soon as you buy them, at your request, so if you withdraw we refund what you paid minus the part for the days you used. Use “Withdraw from contract here” in Settings → Plans, or write to us; we refund you through Google Play within 14 days.
  • Super Likes and Boosts are supplied as soon as you buy them. When you buy one, you ask for it to start straight away and accept that you lose the right to withdraw once it has started; we confirm this by email. Refunds otherwise follow Google Play’s refund policy and the law.
  • We may change what the free and paid plans include, for a valid reason such as new features, security or a change in the law, at no extra cost to you. If a change makes your paid plan noticeably worse, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before, and you can end the plan and get back what you paid for the time left. If we raise a plan’s price, we’ll tell you before it applies, and you can cancel before then.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or the law, as explained in How we moderate.

As far as the law allows, we aren’t responsible for how other members behave, on Blum or off it. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility where the law doesn’t allow it, such as for death or injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app at least 30 days before the change applies. If you don’t agree, you can delete your account, and if you have a paid plan, end it and get back what you paid for the time left.

Law and disputes

These terms are governed by Portuguese law. If you’re a consumer living elsewhere in the EEA, in Switzerland, in the USA or in Canada, you also keep the protection of the laws where you live that can’t be set aside, and you can take a dispute to the courts where you live.

Before going to court, please contact us: most problems can be solved quickly. You can also turn to a consumer arbitration centre in Portugal: CNIACC (www.cniacc.pt), the centre that covers our registered office. You can also use Portugal’s official online complaints book, the Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico, at www.livroreclamacoes.pt.
