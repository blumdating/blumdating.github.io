Blum’s child safety standards

Blum is a dating app only for adults aged 18 or over. These are our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our standards

We don’t allow child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form: not in photos, messages or profiles, and not in any attempt to contact or groom a child. Anyone under 18 is banned from Blum, and sign-up is refused to anyone whose date of birth shows they’re under 18.

How to report

Anyone can report a concern. Members can use Report on any profile or chat and choose the reason Looks under 18.

Without an account, write to our child safety contact at blum.dating.app@gmail.com. A report of child sexual abuse material doesn’t need your name.

Write to our child safety contact

What we do

  • A person reviews every report, and reports that someone looks under 18 are reviewed first.
  • We remove child sexual abuse material as soon as we know about it, and ban the account.
  • We report it to the police and the authorities responsible, including, where the law requires it, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the USA and Cybertip.ca in Canada.
  • We close any account we find belongs to someone under 18.

Child safety contact

Our child safety contact is blum.dating.app@gmail.com. Write there with questions about these standards, or about anything on Blum that involves a child’s safety.

If a child is in danger right now, call 112 (911 in the USA and Canada).

These rules are also part of our Terms of Service.
